$350–$500 typical

  • One-page responsive website
  • Custom visual styling suited to your business
  • Business information and services
  • Clickable phone, email and contact links

Business Website

Custom quote

For businesses that need more detail: several services to explain, work to show off, or questions customers ask again and again. The final price depends on the agreed scope.

  • Multiple sections or pages, depending on scope

Questions

How much does a website cost?

Standard projects typically cost $350–$500. Introductory pricing may start at $100 for a suitable project, depending on its scope; that is not a flat price for every website. More extensive sites are quoted based on scope. We'll agree on the price before work begins. Domain registration, paid hosting and other third-party costs are discussed separately.

How long does a website take to build?

It depends on the project and on how quickly you provide content and feedback. We'll agree on a realistic timeline when we discuss scope.

What do I need to provide?

Your business details, services, contact information, and any logo, photos or text you want used. If you're missing something, we can talk through options.

Can you redesign an existing website?

Yes, if it suits the scope. Send the link and I'll tell you what I'd change and whether it fits one of the packages.

Will my website work on mobile devices?

Yes. Every site is built to be responsive and checked on phone, tablet and desktop sizes.

Do I need to buy a domain?

A custom domain is optional. If you choose one, registration is a separate cost; we can discuss publishing and hosting options before work begins. You should keep ownership and billing for your domain in your own account.

Can you update my website after launch?

Ongoing updates and maintenance aren't included in the packages. If you'd like them, we can discuss that separately.
