Services and pricing

Two starting points. Prices are negotiable, and each project is scoped and priced with you before any work begins.

Starter Website

$350–$500 negotiable

For businesses that need a professional, straightforward online presence: one page that explains who you are, shows what you do and makes contacting you easy.

  • One-page responsive website
  • Custom visual styling suited to your business
  • Business information and services
  • Clickable phone, email and contact links
  • Gallery section where appropriate
  • Basic on-page SEO setup
  • One agreed round of revisions
  • Assistance publishing the site

Business Website

$750–$1,000 negotiable

For businesses that need more detail: several services to explain, work to show off, or questions customers ask again and again.

  • Multiple sections or pages, depending on scope
  • Expanded service descriptions
  • Gallery or portfolio presentation
  • FAQs where useful
  • Contact and booking links
  • Basic on-page SEO setup
  • An agreed number of revisions
  • Assistance publishing the site

These are indicative ranges, and the final price depends on the agreed scope and your budget. As I build my client list, I'm open to reduced introductory pricing for the right project, from $100. Domain registration is optional and we'll discuss it when you inquire about your website. Third-party subscriptions, paid hosting, custom integrations, extensive copywriting and ongoing maintenance are also discussed separately.

Questions

How much does a website cost?

Smaller websites are typically $350–$500 and larger ones $750–$1,000. These ranges are negotiable, and I'm open to reduced introductory pricing for early clients, from $100. We'll agree a price based on your scope before work begins. Domains, paid hosting and other third-party costs are separate.

How long does a website take to build?

It depends on the project and on how quickly you provide content and feedback. We'll agree on a realistic timeline when we discuss scope.

What do I need to provide?

Your business details, services, contact information, and any logo, photos or text you want used. If you're missing something, we can talk through options.

Can you redesign an existing website?

Yes, if it suits the scope. Send the link and I'll tell you what I'd change and whether it fits one of the packages.

Will my website work on mobile devices?

Yes. Every site is built to be responsive and checked on phone, tablet and desktop sizes.

Do I need to buy a domain?

A custom domain is optional and bought separately. We'll discuss that step when you inquire about your website, and I can help you publish on a free option first.

Can you update my website after launch?

Ongoing updates and maintenance aren't included in the packages. If you'd like them, we can discuss that separately.
